# Visual plan self-review rubric (content only)

Do this yourself, in your own reasoning, before returning.

Score your own draft 1-10 on each of: source_fidelity,
generation_mode_appropriateness, visual_coherence, narrative_alignment,
internal_consistency.

Scale: 1 = very poor, 5 = mediocre/needs work, 8 = strong and
production-ready, 9 = excellent, 10 = exceptional. Score honestly -- an
honest low score simply triggers another self-correction pass on your next
attempt; a falsely inflated score would ship a visual plan that actually
fails source fidelity or consistency downstream.

Judge especially:
- SOURCE FIDELITY: apply the visual-plan checks in the source-fidelity-content
  skill.
- GENERATION MODE APPROPRIATENESS: the video nominations, and their order,
  are the beats that genuinely gain most from real motion, each with a
  video_motion_intent from the reliable list that names no camera move -- and
  every shot still carries usable still_motion in case its nomination is not
  taken up.
- VISUAL COHERENCE: overall_visual_style is the supplied style guide,
  unchanged, every shot suits it, and the shots read as one world, not a
  patchwork. Nothing in the plan describes the main person's looks.
- NARRATIVE ALIGNMENT: each picture actually shows its scene's beat, so a
  viewer who hears the line and sees the frame gets the same idea.
- INTERNAL CONSISTENCY: shot sequence, asset references, visual_treatment,
  and shows_character (true exactly where the main person is the focal
  subject) are all mutually consistent.
- DRAWABILITY: no frame_composition or motion_plan needs readable text,
  numbers, or a diagram to make sense. Count this under narrative_alignment.

If any dimension falls short, revise the draft yourself -- within this same
session, without asking anyone -- and re-score, repeating until you
genuinely believe every score is at least 8, source_fidelity is at least 9,
and internal_consistency is at least 9, or until further revision within
this session stops helping.
