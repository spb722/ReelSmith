VISUAL PLAN

OVERALL APPROACH
A grounded, reflective visual journey that leverages the existing textured illustrations. The edit will rely on slow, deliberate camera movements (Ken Burns style) to match the philosophical, mature tone of the narration, allowing the viewer time to absorb the concepts.

VISUAL TONE
Reflective, Melancholic, Philosophical, Calm, Introspective

EDITING STYLE
Slow-paced and deliberate. Cuts should be soft and motivated by shifts in the narrative from concrete story to abstract philosophy. The rhythm should feel like a deep breath.

STYLE GUIDE
- Format: 9:16 Vertical
- Camera motion: Slow pushes, subtle pans, and gentle drifts. No sudden movements or snap zooms. Motion should feel like a steady, guiding hand.
- Transitions: Soft crossfades (15-24 frames) to maintain a dreamlike, reflective flow. Hard cuts should only be used if a stark contrast is needed.
- Text treatment: Elegant serif or clean, thin sans-serif for impact text. Impact text should fade in smoothly and sit comfortably within the negative space of the compositions.
- Background/texture: Preserve the halftone dot patterns and visible textures of the original art. Do not over-sharpen or denoise.
- Color/contrast: Enhance the warm oranges and purples to create a cohesive color grade across the different illustrations. Keep the shadows lifted to maintain a soft, vintage feel.
